# Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

This policy covers the SimpleDiagrams diagram skill provided by
McQuillen Interactive Pty. Ltd.

The skill helps an AI agent construct a SimpleDiagrams document from your
description or a diagram image. Its Python scripts read the diagram spec and
bundled shape catalogue, check the document, and optionally write a new document
or preview. They do not read credentials, upload your files, or collect analytics.
We do not receive or retain your prompts, source files or generated documents
through the skill.

The AI application you choose handles your prompts, attachments, conversation
and execution environment under its own privacy policy. Processing may happen
on your computer or in that provider's hosted environment. Generated files
remain in that environment until removed by you or the provider. Installing
Python dependencies may contact a package repository; the diagram scripts
themselves make no network requests.

If you choose to send us information for support, our
[SimpleDiagrams privacy policy](https://simplediagrams.com/legal/privacy/)
explains how we handle it and how to contact us about privacy questions.
